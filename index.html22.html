I research tech stories the way an editor needs them: every claim traced to a source and labelled by how well it holds up. I also build AI automation and Python tools, so I can read technical sources critically.

How I verify a claim

  1. Find the primary source first: the company release, filing, court record or study.
  2. Cross-check against at least two independent, reputable outlets.
  3. Record the claim, source, date and status in a tracker.
  4. Flag single-sourced or disputed claims, and mark allegations as reported rather than stated as fact.

Projects

Research Radar (AI automation)

A Python pipeline that monitors tech news feeds, groups coverage of the same story across outlets, extracts claims with the Claude API and writes a digest to verify. It never marks a claim as confirmed: a human checks the primary source.

claimlog (Python)

A command-line source tracker that logs each claim with its source, date and verification status, and exports to CSV or Markdown. Includes input validation and unit tests.
